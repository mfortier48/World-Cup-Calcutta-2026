# World Cup 2026 Calcutta Pool Rules

## League Setup

- The pool uses all 48 World Cup 2026 teams.
- Each team is auctioned individually.
- There are 12 owners.
- Each owner has a hard $150 spending cap.
- Owners do not need to spend the full $150.
- The amount actually spent in the auction becomes the full prize pot.
- Every team must be purchased before the tournament starts.
- Shared ownership is allowed only if recorded before kickoff.

## Auction Draw

- The auction can be run from a shuffled 52-card deck.
- 48 cards represent the 48 teams.
- 4 cards are Social/Joker cards.
- When a Social/Joker card is drawn, everyone takes a drink together and no team is auctioned for that card.
- Jokers may be returned to the deck if the league chooses to keep the social cards live.

## Payout Formula

The full prize pot is allocated as follows:

| Category | Share |
| --- | ---: |
| Group-stage wins | 17% |
| Group-stage draws | 3% |
| Round of 32: group winners | 4.5% |
| Round of 32: group runners-up | 3.5% |
| Round of 32: third-place qualifiers | 2% |
| Reach Round of 16 | 12% |
| Reach Quarterfinals | 12% |
| Reach Semifinals | 12% |
| Reach Final | 8% |
| Champion | 12% |
| Most goals scored | 4% |
| Best goal differential | 4% |
| Biggest single-match upset | 3% |
| Worst goal differential | 3% |

Total: 100%.

## Group-Stage Payouts

- The 17% group-stage wins pool is divided by total team wins in the group stage.
- Each team win earns that team's owner one share of the wins pool.
- The 3% group-stage draws pool is divided by total team draws in the group stage.
- Each team draw earns that team's owner one share of the draws pool.

## Round Of 32 Advancement Payouts

The Round of 32 advancement payout is a 10% total pool split into three mini-pools:

- 4.5% of the full prize pot is split equally among the 12 group winners.
- 3.5% of the full prize pot is split equally among the 12 group runners-up.
- 2% of the full prize pot is split equally among the 8 third-place qualifiers.

This keeps the total Round of 32 payout at 10%, while giving a small bonus for better group-stage performance.

## Knockout Advancement Payouts

- 12% is split equally among the 16 teams that reach the Round of 16.
- 12% is split equally among the 8 teams that reach the Quarterfinals.
- 12% is split equally among the 4 teams that reach the Semifinals.
- 8% is split equally among the 2 teams that reach the Final.
- 12% is paid to the Champion.

These advancement payouts stack. For example, the Champion earns the Round of 32 payout, Round of 16 payout, Quarterfinal payout, Semifinal payout, Final payout, and Champion payout.

## Side Pots

### Most Goals Scored

- Individual team award.
- The team with the most total goals scored across the tournament wins this side pot.
- If multiple teams tie, they split the side pot.
- This is not cumulative across an owner's full portfolio.

### Best Goal Differential

- Individual team award.
- Goal differential is goals for minus goals against for one team across the tournament.
- The team with the best positive goal differential wins.
- If multiple teams tie, they split the side pot.
- This is not cumulative across an owner's full portfolio.

### Biggest Single-Match Upset

- Individual team award.
- An upset is when a lower-ranked team beats a higher-ranked team.
- The upset gap is winner FIFA ranking minus loser FIFA ranking.
- Example: Haiti (83) beating Argentina (1) would score an upset gap of 82.
- The largest upset gap wins.
- If multiple teams tie, they split the side pot.
- Rankings for this award are locked to FIFA's official June 11, 2026 men's ranking table and do not change during the tournament.

### Worst Goal Differential

- Individual team award.
- The team with the worst total goal differential across the tournament wins this side pot.
- If multiple teams tie, they split the side pot.
- This is not cumulative across an owner's full portfolio.

## FIFA Rankings

- The ranking shown in parentheses next to each team is its latest visible FIFA men's world ranking and may be refreshed during the tournament.
- Biggest-upset calculations always use the rankings locked at tournament kickoff from FIFA's official June 11, 2026 table.
- Later ranking updates cannot retroactively change an earlier upset result.

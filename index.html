Pool Rules

  • All 48 teams are auctioned individually.
  • Each owner has a hard $150 spending cap.
  • Owners do not need to spend the full budget.
  • The amount actually spent becomes the entire prize pot.
  • Every team must be purchased before the tournament starts.
  • Shared ownership is allowed only if recorded before kickoff.
  • The Round of 32 advancement pool is tiered by group finish: group winners, group runners-up, and third-place qualifiers are paid from separate mini-pools.

Side-Pot Definitions

How The Fun Payouts Work

Most goals scored
Individual team award. The team with the most total goals scored across the tournament wins this side pot. If multiple teams tie, they split it.
Best goal differential
Individual team award. Goal differential is goals for minus goals against for a single team across the tournament. Best positive differential wins; ties split.
Biggest single-match upset
Individual team award. An upset is when a lower-ranked team beats a higher-ranked team. The upset gap is winner FIFA rank minus loser FIFA rank, so Haiti (83) beating Argentina (1) would score 82. Largest upset gap wins; ties split.
Worst goal differential
Individual team award. This is the single team with the worst total goal differential across the tournament. It is not cumulative across an owner's portfolio. Ties split.

FIFA rankings are shown in parentheses next to team names and use FIFA's men's world ranking page as the source of truth. The top 10 reflect the current FIFA ranking page, where Argentina is #1; the full list will be checked again after FIFA's next official update and before the first matches on Thursday, June 11, 2026.
